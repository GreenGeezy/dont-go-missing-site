Questions

Straight answers before you head out.

What is Don't Go Missing?

Don't Go Missing is a safety app for iPhone and Android that makes sure someone knows where you went and when you were due back. You build a trip plan in about 90 seconds, text it to people you trust, and the app reminds you to check in and sounds an alarm on your phone if you are overdue.

Is Don't Go Missing available for iPhone and Android?

It is made for both. The iPhone app is on the App Store and needs iOS 16 or later. The Android app is live on Google Play under the package name com.dontgomissing.app. On both, you build a trip plan, send it to people you trust, and get check-in reminders and overdue alarms on your phone.

Does Don't Go Missing work without cell service?

Yes for everything on your phone: check-in reminders, the return-time alarm, overdue alerts and the GPS trail are stored and scheduled on the device, so they work with no signal. Sending a text message to your contacts, including an SOS, needs cell service or Wi-Fi.

Does the app automatically alert my contacts if I am overdue?

No. The app has no servers and does not send text messages silently on iPhone or Android. You review and send each message yourself. Your contacts receive your plan before you leave, including the exact time to start worrying and what to do. If that time passes without hearing from you, they act. Your phone also alerts you so you can check in, extend, or send a running-late text.

Is Don't Go Missing a replacement for a satellite messenger or PLB?

No. A satellite messenger or personal locator beacon can call for help from places with no cell coverage, and this app cannot. Don't Go Missing covers the other half of the problem: making sure someone knows your plan and notices when you are late. Use both on remote trips.

How is it different from sharing my location with Find My or Life360?

Live location sharing stops updating the moment you lose signal, and it does not tell anyone when you were supposed to be back. Don't Go Missing gives your contacts a plan with a destination, a return time and a deadline to act, and it keeps a GPS trail on your phone while you are offline.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone or Android phone. There is no account and no cloud sync. Your trips, contacts and GPS trail stay on the device unless you choose to send a message. Read the Privacy Policy.

Does the Android app track my location in the background?

Only during an active journey that you start. The Android app uses a foreground location service to record your GPS trail, shows a persistent notification the whole time, and stops when you end the journey. The trail is stored only on your device.

How much does Don't Go Missing cost?

US prices depend on the store. On iPhone, Don't Go Missing is $4.99 per week or $39.99 per year, billed by Apple, and the yearly plan starts with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. On Android, it is $4.99 per week or $39 per year, billed by Google Play, and the yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. A free trial becomes a paid subscription unless you cancel before it ends.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel in the store where you subscribed. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Is Don't Go Missing an emergency service?

No. It is a planning and alerting tool, not an emergency service, and it cannot guarantee rescue. In an emergency with signal, call your local emergency number first.
