Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Don't Go Missing

Effective Date: May 26, 2026
Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Who We Are

Don't Go Missing is a wilderness safety planning app for iOS and Android, used to create Safe Journeys before hikes, hunts, fishing trips, camps, remote drives, and other off-grid travel. This policy covers the iOS app on the App Store, the Android app on Google Play (both with the app ID com.dontgomissing.app), and the website at dontgomissing.com.

What the App Does

The app helps users plan destinations, set return times, choose trusted contacts, create safety checklists, prepare check-ins, share route or trip details through the sharing tools built into iOS and Android, and prepare SOS messages when needed. Don't Go Missing is not an emergency service and is not a replacement for emergency services.

Information You Provide

You may choose to enter an emergency contact name or phone number, trip destination, return time, check-in settings, and related safety planning details such as vehicle or gear information. No account is required. If you contact us for support, feedback, or a data deletion request, we may receive your email address and the information you include in that message.

Location Information

Location information may be used to show maps, plan journeys, prepare SOS messages, and support journey tracking when you enable those features. Location accuracy depends on your device, settings, terrain, signal, and other conditions. We do not operate a server that receives your location, and we do not use location for advertising or analytics. You can change location permission at any time in your device settings.

On iOS: the app may request When In Use and Always location permissions depending on the features you choose to use, including background journey tracking.

On Android: the app uses an Android Foreground Location Service to record your GPS trail. It runs only during an active journey that you start, and stops when you end the journey. While it runs, Android shows a persistent notification so you always know location is in use. The recorded location is stored strictly on your device. It leaves your device only if you choose to share it, for example by sending a message that includes your coordinates.

Local and On-Device Storage

Trip, contact, checklist, check-in, journey, and GPS trail data is stored locally on your device. The app has no account and no cloud sync of its own.

Purchases and Subscriptions

On iOS, Apple and the App Store handle purchases, billing, refunds, subscription management, and cancellation. On Android, Google Play Billing handles purchases, billing, refunds, subscription management, and cancellation, including free trials. We never receive your full payment card details.

On both platforms we use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat processes your purchase history (such as the product, purchase date, and renewal or expiry status) and an anonymous app user ID. The app does not send RevenueCat your name, trips, contacts, or GPS trail. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Local notifications may be used for check-ins, return-time reminders, overdue alarms, and safety planning prompts if you allow notifications on your device. On Android, an active journey also shows the persistent notification described above.

Sharing and SMS

On iOS, the app may use the standard iOS share sheet and MessageUI/SMS composer. On Android, the app may use the standard Android Share Sheet and open your phone's SMS composer. On both platforms these only prepare a plan or message. You review it, choose who receives it, and send it yourself. The app does not send messages silently. Once sent, a message is handled by the messaging app or service you choose and by its recipients.

Website Privacy

This website is static. It uses no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking scripts. The free tools on this website, such as the trip plan generator, run entirely in your browser. What you type is not sent to us. The trip plan generator saves your draft in your browser's local storage on your own device so you can reuse it, and you can remove it at any time with the Clear form button.

No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising tracking.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data in two ways, on iOS and Android:

  • In the app: open Settings and clear your local app data. This removes the trip, contact, and journey data the app stores on your device. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data from the device.
  • By email: write to dontgomissingapp@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request." We will delete support emails and, where we can match it to your request, the RevenueCat record linked to your anonymous app user ID. Tell us your platform and, if your request involves a purchase, the store order number. Never send payment card details.

We may keep limited information where the law requires it. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own policies. Deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play, as explained in our Terms of Use.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Safety Disclaimer

Don't Go Missing is not emergency services. Do not rely on this app as your only safety measure. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Terms of Use

Use of the app and website is subject to our Terms of Use. The iOS app is licensed under the Apple Standard EULA.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here and update the Last Updated date above.
