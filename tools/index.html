Free tools

Free hiking safety tools

Three things to do before any trip: know the place, work out when you'll be back, and tell someone. Every tool runs in your browser. No sign-up, and nothing is uploaded.

New free tool

National Park Missing Persons Map

Search any National Park, National Forest or state to see documented missing-person cases, why solo visitors lose contact there, and what to do before you go.

Check a park → Free tool

Hiking Trip Plan Generator

Enter where you're going and when you'll be back. Get a clear plan with a deadline for calling for help, ready to text, email or print for someone you trust.

Make a trip plan → Free tool

Hiking Time & Turnaround Calculator

Estimate how long a hike will take from distance and elevation gain using Naismith's rule, and see the latest time to turn around so you're out before dark.

Calculate hiking time →
